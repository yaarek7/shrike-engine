# M7 verification: draw-aware search state

- Date: 2026-09-28

## Correctness coverage

The milestone uses full Zobrist recomputation as an independent oracle for incremental updates.
The move suite checks every legal child of positions containing ordinary moves, all castling
rights, legal en passant, captures, and promotions. Every child key must equal recomputation, and
unmake must restore both the original key and the complete position.

Focused tests additionally establish:

- move clocks do not alter repetition identity;
- legal en passant alters identity, while uncapturable and king-pinned en passant do not;
- history passed through the public API gives a losing side a zero-valued third-occurrence claim;
- UCI retains all keys produced by `position ... moves` and detects a repeated starting position;
- mate in one is selected over both third-occurrence and 100-ply claim options;
- losing positions with a claim score zero while still returning a legal root move;
- fivefold repetition and the 150-ply threshold are distinct automatic draws;
- conservative insufficient-material cases are automatic draws;
- checkmate takes precedence over repetition and move-count rules; terminal move detection also
  runs before the conservative material rule;
- UCI never emits `bestmove 0000` solely because a claim is available.
